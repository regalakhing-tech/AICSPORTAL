Program Coverage

AICS Assistance Programs

Choose an assistance category below to check requirements and apply online.

Healthcare Aid

Medical Assistance

Direct financial aid for hospital bills, medicines, lab tests, and treatments.

Eligibility: Patients, seniors, PWDs, and low-income Antique residents.
Coverage: Hospital bills, diagnostic tests, medications, and dialysis.
Apply for Medical Aid

Required Documents to Upload

  • Valid ID: Government photo ID or Barangay Certificate.
  • Medical Certificate: Clinical abstract signed by your doctor.
  • Billing / Prescription: Hospital bill or official pharmacy prescription.
Upload clear photos using your phone camera (JPG, PNG, PDF).
Funeral Aid

Burial Assistance

Financial support to help bereaved families cover funeral and burial costs.

Eligibility: Immediate surviving family members living in Antique.
Coverage: Casket, mortuary services, and burial expenses.
Apply for Burial Aid

Required Documents to Upload

  • Valid ID: Government photo ID of the claimant.
  • Death Certificate: Registered copy from Civil Registrar or hospital.
  • Funeral Contract: Official receipt or contract from funeral home.
Upload clear photos using your phone camera (JPG, PNG, PDF).
Special Welfare Aid

Special Cases

Emergency assistance for individuals facing acute personal, family, or protective situations.

Eligibility: Individuals in severe crisis, distress, or emergency displacement.
Coverage: Temporary shelter, emergency transportation, and crisis sustenance.
Apply for Special Cases

Required Documents to Upload

  • Valid ID: Government photo ID of applicant or claimant.
  • Police / Medical Report: Official police blotter or hospital medical report.
Upload clear photos using your phone camera (JPG, PNG, PDF).
Basic Education Aid

Educational Assistance - Basic Education

Educational allowances and cash subsidies for elementary and junior/senior high school students in Antique.

Eligibility: Enrolled basic education students from low-income or solo-parent families.
Coverage: Tuition fees, books, school supplies, uniforms, and student allowances.
Apply for Educational Aid

Required Documents to Upload

  • Valid ID: Parent, guardian, or student government ID.
  • School ID: The student's school ID card.
  • Enrollment Form: The enrollment or registration form for the current school year.
Upload clear photos using your phone camera (JPG, PNG, PDF).
Calamity & Disaster Aid

Disaster Victims

Emergency financial relief for families affected by typhoon, flood, fire, or natural calamity.

Eligibility: Families with damaged homes or displacement in declared calamity areas.
Coverage: Shelter repair assistance, food packs, and basic relief essentials.
Apply for Disaster Relief

Required Documents to Upload

  • Valid ID: Household head government photo ID.
  • Disaster Certificate: MDRRMO or Barangay calamity certificate.
  • House Photo: Photo showing damage to your residential home.
Upload clear photos using your phone camera (JPG, PNG, PDF).
Economic Relief

Persons Affected by High Inflation Rate

Direct financial subsidy and food commodity assistance for low-income families burdened by rising market prices.

Eligibility: Minimum-wage earners, informal workers, and low-income families in Antique.
Coverage: Emergency grocery aid, essential food commodities, and household sustenance.
Apply for Inflation Relief

Required Documents to Upload

  • Valid ID: Government photo ID (PhilSys National ID, Driver's License, or Barangay Certificate).
Upload clear photos using your phone camera (JPG, PNG, PDF).
